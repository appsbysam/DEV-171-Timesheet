BUG FIX
-------
• Fixed Copy Previous Week searching for 26/07/2026 instead of 27/07/2026.
• The fault was caused by converting a local midnight date to UTC with toISOString(), which moved the date back one day in Sydney and other positive UTC time zones.
• Date calculations now use UTC-safe year, month and day values.

COPY PREVIOUS WEEK
------------------
• The currently selected Week Starting date remains the destination.
• The source week is now exactly seven calendar days before that selected date.
• Example:
  Destination: 03/08/2026
  Source:      27/07/2026
• The unnecessary Sunday fallback has been removed.
