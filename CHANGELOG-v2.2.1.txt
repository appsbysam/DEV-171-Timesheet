IMPROVEMENTS
------------
• Moved the Add Split Shift button onto the same line as the staff member's name.
• When a split shift is active, the Remove Split Shift button now appears beside the staff member's name.
• Reorganised each employee row into a cleaner vertical layout:
  - Staff name and split-shift action at the top
  - Shift 1 times underneath
  - Optional Shift 2 times underneath
  - Daily total positioned at the bottom
• Improved spacing and alignment on mobile screens.
• Updated the print layout to match the new arrangement.

BUG FIXES
---------
• Fixed the completed-shift green highlighting disappearing when a split shift was added.
• Completed split-shift rows now retain the same green highlight as normal completed rows.

VERSION MANAGEMENT
------------------
• Updated version.js to 2.2.1.
• Updated manifest.json version metadata to 2.2.1.
• The displayed version and cache-busting continue to update automatically from version.js.
