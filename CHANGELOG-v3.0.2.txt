Version 3.0.2

URGENT WEEK-DATE FIX
--------------------
• A work week now always starts on Monday.
• A work week now always ends on Saturday.
• Sunday is never used as a Week Starting date.
• On Sunday, the app selects the following Monday.
• Monday through Saturday select the Monday within that operating week.

ROOT CAUSE
----------
• JavaScript toISOString() converts local dates to UTC.
• In Sydney and other positive UTC time zones, local Monday midnight can become Sunday in UTC.
• The app now formats calendar dates using local year, month and day values instead.

ADDITIONAL DATE SAFETY
----------------------
• Previous Week and Next Week now use UTC-safe date-only arithmetic.
• Week Ending is calculated as exactly five days after Monday.
• Manager Date now uses the local calendar date and cannot shift back one day.

EXAMPLES
--------
• Sunday 02/08/2026 → Week Starting 03/08/2026
• Monday 03/08/2026 → Week Starting 03/08/2026
• Saturday 08/08/2026 → Week Starting 03/08/2026
• Week Ending for 03/08/2026 → 08/08/2026
